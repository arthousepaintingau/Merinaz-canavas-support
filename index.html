A drawing course for iPad. No account required.

Merinaz Academy teaches drawing in structured lessons: theory, a demonstration, practice you can score, unit exams, and a certificate kept on your iPad. The earlier colouring-template studio has been removed.

Getting started

  • On first launch, enter a name if you want one on your certificate, or skip it, then choose a level.
  • On the Course tab, tap Start Lesson. Read the short theory, watch the stages, then draw. Check Drawing scores the attempt and shows the target over your lines.
  • Practice holds a daily warm-up. Sketchbook is a blank page: choose the paper, then draw. Portfolio keeps drawings you have checked.
  • Settings, the gear, is where you change your name and level, choose finger or Apple Pencil, and reset course progress.

Drawing

Lessons are made for Apple Pencil, including pressure, tilt, and double-tap or squeeze for the eraser. A finger can draw as well. Two fingers pinch to zoom the sheet. On a lesson, the expand control enlarges the sheet to the screen.

Contact
